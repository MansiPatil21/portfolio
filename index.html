README.md

My undergraduate specialization was cybersecurity and forensics, which is where I learned to think about failure first: what happens when a request is malformed, when two writers race for the same row, when a node stops answering. That habit carries into everything below — I'd rather reproduce a bug and prove the fix than assume a passing test means the system is correct.

corebank — removed the row lock, re-ran the test, watched 11 transfers clear against a balance that should only cover 10. Put the lock back, watched it hold.

Currently finishing a Master of Applied Computer Science at Dalhousie University (GPA 3.99), after a B.Tech in Computer Science & Engineering from MIT World Peace University (CGPA 8.18). Looking for a co-op starting January 2027.
